Roll · Mask · Generate

Two stages that meet at one picture. Upstairs you cut the tile somewhere else and paint the part you want redrawn pure white. Downstairs FLUX.2 is handed that picture as an ordinary reference and told to fill the white in. No mask channel and no inpainting model: the white hole is the instruction.

1 · Reference studio — rolling and masking happen here in the browser; the GPU never sees this part

Drop the picture to work on, or click to choose

Sides are rounded to a multiple of 16 (one token). The picture is cover-cropped to fit, never squashed.

Roll — drag the window

Four copies of the tile; the bright window is the tile you keep. Slide it and the tile's own edges — the green lines — move into the open, where they can be painted over and redrawn.

Mask — paint what the model should redraw

This one file is the whole hand-off: drop it back in later and the run repeats.

2 · What to do with it

System prompt — the turn in front of every prompt

Changing this re-encodes the prompt (a second or two on the first use of each wording); after that it is cached. It is part of the conditioning, so the same prompt under two system turns is two different runs.

Settings

Turning seamless off sets the seamless strength to 0 and leaves an ordinary FLUX.2 edit — which is what you want when the picture is not a tile. Prompt strength 1 is the distilled models' fixed value, and it costs nothing: the unconditional branch drops out of every step.

Advanced

Reference detail caps the reference's area. Below the tile's own size it is resampled, which softens the white edges the prompt is pointing at; above it costs attention tokens and nothing else.
